♡ Gllitie Bot

Your cutesy multi-functional Discord bot with economy, pets, mini-games, and automated drops!

♡ Features & Highlights

• 56 Commands Across 8 Categories: Full coverage including Economy, Shop, Events, Embeds, Autoresponders, Settings, Administration, and Pets & Activities.

• Dual Slash & Prefix Support: Use standard /command or prefix .command anywhere.

• Interactive /commands Directory: Gorgeous light blue interface with a dropdown category selector and return navigation.

• Pet Progression System: Start with Gllitie (Free 1.0x) and unlock pets like Yuna, Irohi, Minji, Moka, and Woni for massive multipliers!
